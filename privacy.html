Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy covers the Kessler Cascade iOS app. In short: Kessler Cascade does not collect, store or share any personal information about you.

Information we collect

None. Kessler Cascade has no accounts and no sign-up. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools, and we don't receive any data from your device.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your high scores, bonus stars, unlocked level packs and settings (such as the sound and music toggles) locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center

Kessler Cascade uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple and handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. Your Game Center profile, such as your name and avatar, is controlled by Apple and your Game Center settings. We don't receive or store it. You can use the game without signing in to Game Center.

In-app purchases

Level packs are bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never see your payment details, name or Apple ID, and only learn from Apple that a purchase was made so the pack can be unlocked on your device.

Children

Kessler Cascade doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. The current version is always available at this page.
